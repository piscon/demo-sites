Industrial Valve & Centrifugal Pump Manufacturer in East China

Based in Lingang Industrial Park, Dongzhou, the company occupies 54 mu of land with 36,000 m2 of workshops and 186 employees, including 34 technical staff. Since our in-house foundry came online in 2019, valve bodies and bonnets have been cast and machined entirely in-house, keeping lead times and quality under our own control. Our four product lines - gate valves, butterfly valves, ball valves and centrifugal pumps - serve the petrochemical, municipal water supply, power generation and water treatment industries. Exports began in 2016, mainly to Southeast Asia and the Middle East, accounting for just over 20% of sales. Every valve passes a seat leakage test before dispatch, with the test record travelling with the goods; every pump is performance-tested on our test stand, with the curve attached behind the certificate of conformity.
