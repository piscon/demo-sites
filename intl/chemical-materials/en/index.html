About Us

Engineering Plastics & Specialty Resin Manufacturer

Founded in 2012, Hengtuo New Materials occupies 68,000 m² with 22 twin-screw extrusion lines and a 10,000-tonne resin synthesis unit, producing 65,000 tonnes of modified engineering plastics and specialty resins a year. Certified to ISO 9001 and IATF 16949, our products serve the auto-parts, electronics, rail-transit and medical-device industries, with a performance database of 3,000+ grades open for selection queries.

Why Choose Us!

Why Choose HENGTUO NEW MATERIALS

What new materials fear most is batch drift. These are the three reasons clients write us into their AVL approved-vendor lists.

Batch Variation ≤ ±1%

Key indicators controlled internally to ±1%, retain samples kept for 24 months batch by batch — your incoming-inspection data can be reconciled line by line.

QR Traceability on Every Batch

Every bag carries a batch QR code — raw-material source, production line and test results in one scan.

Open Materials Database

A performance database of 3,000+ grades open for queries; when replacing imported materials, we send samples for validation before you switch.
