What a tune-up covers

The checks behind every tune-up

Each tune-up takes 60 to 90 minutes. You get a printed checklist with the readings, so you can see the system change from one season to the next.

Spring cooling tune-up
  • Refrigerant pressures and temperature split
  • Outdoor coil rinse and fin straightening
  • Capacitor, contactor and fan motor amps
  • Condensate drain flush and safety switch test
  • Thermostat calibration and cooling cycle test
Fall heating tune-up
  • Burner, ignitor and flame sensor service
  • Heat exchanger inspection
  • Flue draft and carbon monoxide readings
  • Gas pressure and limit switch checks
  • Heat pump defrost and backup heat test
