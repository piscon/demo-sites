Tiller — Free Bootstrap 5 Agriculture Company Website Template by uiCookies
https://uicookies.com/

Tiller is a free website template for agricultural services companies and
farmers' co-operatives, built on Bootstrap 5. It presents the fictional Tiller
Agri Co-op across three pages: a home page with a depot status board, services
grid, commodity price board, yield and input calculator, sustainability stats,
member benefits, agronomist team, field days and a call-back form; a services
page with process steps for each service and a soil-test booking form; and a
machinery hire catalogue with filters, member and standard rates, and a hire
quote dialog. The design pairs deep field green, wheat gold and dark soil with
Archivo headings and Barlow body text. Everything is plain vanilla JavaScript
with no jQuery; charts are hand-built inline SVG, all content is visible without
JavaScript, and animations respect prefers-reduced-motion.
Free for personal and commercial use. Attribution is optional but always welcome — https://uicookies.com/license/

WHAT'S INSIDE
  index.html            Home page (depot board, services, price board,
                        calculator, stats, membership, team, news, contact)
  services.html         Service details, process steps, soil-test booking, FAQ
  machinery.html        Hire catalogue with filters, rates and quote dialog
  css/vendor/           Bootstrap 5.3.8 (minified, self-hosted)
  css/style.css         Template design layer (colour and type tokens at the top)
  js/vendor/            Bootstrap 5.3.8 bundle (includes Popper)
  js/main.js            Template behaviour — vanilla JavaScript, no jQuery
  img/                  Demo photos (public domain, see CREDITS.txt)
  CREDITS.txt           Source and licence for every demo photo

NOTES
  - Built on Bootstrap 5.3.8. No jQuery, no plugin dependencies.
  - Colours, fonts and radii are CSS custom properties at the top of
    css/style.css, so a rebrand starts there.
  - Commodity prices, the 15-day price history and the calculator's crop and
    fertiliser figures live in small data objects at the top of js/main.js.
    All prices and figures are illustrative.
  - Machinery cards carry their rates in data attributes (data-daily,
    data-weekly); the filters, the standard-rate switch and the quote estimate
    read from those.
  - Forms are front-end only: they validate in the browser and show a
    confirmation, but nothing is sent. Point them at your backend or a form
    service to make them live.
  - Replace the demo photos in img/ with your own before launch.
