Ductwise — Free HVAC Website Template by uiCookies
https://uicookies.com/

Ductwise is a free website template for heating, ventilation and air-conditioning
contractors, built on Bootstrap 5. It ships five pages for a fictional company,
Ductwise Heating & Air, with a graphite and sheet-metal look, a temperature-scale
accent from glacier blue to ember orange, Archivo Expanded headings and IBM Plex
Sans body text. A heating/cooling season switch changes the hero, emergency line,
call-to-action and accent colour, and picks a default from the current month.
Everything is plain vanilla JavaScript with no jQuery: the season switch, plan
billing toggle, system size and cost estimator, repair-or-replace check, booking
form with live summary, office-hours status and form validation. Content stays
visible without JavaScript and motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
Free for personal and commercial use. Attribution is optional but always welcome — https://uicookies.com/license/

WHAT'S INSIDE

                        how a visit works, plans, financing, certifications,
                        reviews, FAQ
  services.html         AC, furnaces & boilers, heat pumps, ductwork & indoor air,
                        smart thermostats, plus a repair-or-replace check

  js/vendor/            Bootstrap 5.3.8 bundle (includes Popper)

NOTES
  - Built on Bootstrap 5. No jQuery, no plugin dependencies.
  - Colours live at the top of css/style.css. --heat and --cool set the two
    seasonal accents; --accent follows the season switch automatically.
  - Seasonal copy is marked with the season-heat and season-cool classes. The
    heating version shows when JavaScript is off.
  - The size estimator and repair-or-replace check are rules of thumb for
    budgeting. Adjust the rates and example prices in the data objects inside
    initSizer() and initRepairReplace() in js/main.js.
  - Forms are front-end only; point them at your backend, booking software or a
    form service to make them live.
  - All names, prices, licence numbers, reviews and phone numbers are fictional.
  - Replace the demo images in img/ with your own optimized photos before launch.
