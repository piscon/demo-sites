Questions

常见问题

Pricing, timing and what to expect. If your question is not here, the office picks up Monday to Saturday.

Contact the office
How fast can you get here if I have no heat or no cooling?

Emergency calls go straight to the technician on duty. Last winter our average arrival time was 52 minutes inside and under 90 minutes across the rest of the valley. Members are dispatched first.

Do you charge to come out?

A repair diagnostic is $89, and it is credited in full toward any repair you approve. Estimates for a new system are free and include a room-by-room load calculation and written options.

How often should my system be serviced?

Once a year for a furnace or boiler, once a year for central air, and twice a year for a heat pump because it runs in both seasons. Spring and early fall are the easiest times to book.

What size system does my home need?

It depends on the house, not just its square footage. Our size estimate gives a rough range, and a free in-home visit replaces it with a proper load calculation before you buy anything.

Do you work on every make of equipment?

是的。我们自有工厂与实验室，关键部件自制，不绑定单一供应商，推荐给您的永远是最适合您工况的方案。

Can I change my own air filter?

Please do. Check it monthly and replace it every one to three months. On your first visit we will mark the filter size on the cabinet and show you which way the arrow points.
